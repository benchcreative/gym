Progression rules
Starred lifts (★) — add 2.5kg when you hit the top of the rep range across all sets for 2 sessions in a row.

Accessory lifts — add a rep before you add weight. Once you consistently hit the top of the range, add the smallest increment available.

Arms — prioritise clean form and full range of motion over weight. The numbers will follow.

Legs (week 2–3) — leg press and lying leg curl will be added once the upper body structure is bedded in.
